About Us

The original members of The Household recognized a crucial need within the Nigerian male community in South Africa – a desire for genuine friendship and a close-knit brotherhood that they believed could be met through the establishment of such a group.

Our Vision

To create a supportive and inclusive space where men can cultivate profound connections, inspiring one another as well as members embark on a journey of personal and collective growth and success.

Our Core Values

Respect, integrity, excellence, discipline, ensuring a duty of care to all members and ensuring all present and future members receive fair and equal treatment. These are some of the core values that are upheld.

Exclusive Membership Benefits

Exclusive Supportive Community:

Join a vibrant community of Nigerian men based in South Africa. Experience the camaraderie of like-minded individuals who share similar values and aspirations.

Personalized Assistance During Life's Milestones:

Receive thoughtful support during significant life events. Our club is dedicated to standing by you during moments of joy and sorrow, providing a helping hand when needed.

Financial Support in Times of Need:

The Household Brothers offer discreet financial support to eligible members, ensuring they have assistance during critical times like welcoming a newborn or coping with the loss of a parent. This support is available within our community guidelines and is subject to good financial standing.

Customized Member Benefits:

Beyond financial assistance, members enjoy a range of exclusive benefits tailored to foster personal and professional growth. From networking opportunities to curated events and workshops, our club is dedicated to nurturing your overall well-being.

Respectful Confidentiality:

Rest assured, any support offered by The Household Brothers is handled with utmost discretion and respect for privacy. Your personal matters remain confidential within the club's trusted circle.

Diverse Range of Support Services:

Our club also offers a variety of other supportive services that cater to the holistic well-being of our members. From mentorship programs to wellness initiatives, we aim to support you in multiple facets of life.

Join The HOUSEHOLD BROTHERS

Step 1: Introduction

Connect with an existing Johannesburg-based member of The HOUSEHOLD. This current member, known as your sponsor, will introduce you to the group during our regular gatherings.

Step 2: Membership Nomination

Once introduced, your sponsor will propose your membership to the group. Members will vote on your potential admission into the club. Following the vote, your sponsor will inform you of the decision.

Step 3: Proceeding with Membership Application

If approved by the group, you will be guided to proceed with the membership application. Provide essential personal details and express your commitment to the club's regulations and values.

Step 4: Local Presence

Active participation in Johannesburg events, meetings, and engagements is essential. Residing in Johannesburg ensures your full involvement and benefits from our community.

Step 5: Membership Approval

Upon completing the application and acknowledging the club's guidelines, finalize your membership by paying fees and monthly dues. Full membership is granted upon completion.
