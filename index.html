Who we are

About Us

The original members of The Household recognized a crucial need within the Nigerian male community in South Africa — a desire for genuine friendship and a close-knit brotherhood that they believed could be met through the establishment of such a group.

✦

Our Vision

To create a supportive and inclusive space where men can cultivate profound connections, inspiring one another as members embark on a journey of personal and collective growth and success.

✦

Our Core Values

Respect, integrity, excellence and discipline — ensuring a duty of care to all members, and that every present and future member receives fair and equal treatment.
